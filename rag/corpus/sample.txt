== TechFlow Platform — Help & Policy Documentation ==

--- Section 1: Account & Password ---
To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You will receive a reset link to your registered email address within 5 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available for all account types. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS verification. Hardware keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) are available on Enterprise plans only.

--- Section 2: Subscription & Billing ---
TechFlow offers three subscription tiers: Free, Pro ($29/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). All paid plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 20% discount.

Refund Policy: Pro subscribers may request a full refund within 7 days of their initial purchase. After 7 days, no refunds are issued. Enterprise contracts are governed by individual service agreements. There are no refunds for annual plan renewals.

For billing-related support, customers should submit a ticket at support.techflow.io/billing. Do NOT send sensitive payment information over email. Our billing team can be reached Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM IST.

NOTE: The support contact email john.doe@techflow.io is listed in legacy documentation and has been REDACTED from current records. All billing queries go through the ticketing portal.

--- Section 3: Supported Technologies ---
TechFlow's compute platform natively supports the following programming languages:
- Python 3.9+
- Node.js 18+ (JavaScript/TypeScript)
- Go 1.21+
- Java 17+ (via OpenJDK)
- Rust (experimental support, not recommended for production)

Container orchestration is powered by Kubernetes. Docker images are supported. Helm charts are provided for all managed services.

--- Section 4: Data & Privacy ---
TechFlow complies with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 Type II standards. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Customer data is never shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data retention: logs are retained for 90 days by default; this can be extended to 365 days on Enterprise plans. Data deletion requests are processed within 30 business days.

--- Section 5: SLA & Uptime ---
TechFlow guarantees 99.9% uptime for Pro and Enterprise plans. Scheduled maintenance windows are Sundays between 2 AM – 4 AM UTC and are announced 48 hours in advance via the status page at status.techflow.io.

In the event of an outage lasting more than 30 minutes, affected customers are eligible for a service credit of 10% of their monthly fee, up to a maximum of one month's fee.

--- Section 6: Integrations ---
TechFlow integrates with the following third-party services:
- GitHub and GitLab (CI/CD pipelines)
- Slack and Microsoft Teams (alert notifications)
- Datadog and Prometheus (metrics & monitoring)
- AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage, Azure Blob Storage (data export)
- Jira and Linear (issue tracking)

OAuth 2.0 is used for all third-party authentication. API keys can be generated from the developer console.

--- Section 7: API & Rate Limits ---
The TechFlow REST API follows OpenAPI 3.1 specification. API documentation is available at docs.techflow.io/api.

Rate limits: Free tier — 100 requests/hour. Pro tier — 5,000 requests/hour. Enterprise — custom limits.

Authentication: All API calls require a Bearer token in the Authorization header. Tokens expire after 24 hours and can be refreshed using the /auth/refresh endpoint.

TechFlow's leadership team does not publicly endorse any political party or candidate. Any claims to the contrary are not supported by official company communications.
